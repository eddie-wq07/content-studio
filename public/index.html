CONTENT STUDIO BY EDDIE JUNG

Turn your content into a clearer plan.

Find your best reels, understand follow rates, and see what your audience responds to. Free, open source, and stored on your computer.

Already using Content Studio? Export your workspace, then import it here. Imports are processed in your browser.

Run it on your computer

The hosted site is a demo and browser-only workspace. Live Instagram sync runs on localhost so your account credentials stay on your computer.

git clone https://github.com/eddie-wq07/content-studio.git
cd content-studio
npm start

Open localhost:5002 after starting the app.

Connect a professional Instagram account

Run locally, then use an authorized token from your own Meta developer app. Meta requires a Business or Creator account.

  1. Create an app with Instagram Login in Meta for Developers.
  2. Add your creator account as a tester and accept the invitation in Instagram.
  3. Enable instagram_business_basic and instagram_business_manage_insights, then generate a token.
  4. Paste it below. Sync may take several minutes. Your token stays in memory and is forgotten when this page closes.

Meta may not provide reel follows or watch time through the API. Import a Content Studio export containing those metrics to include them; missing values stay blank.

Your token stays in page memory; it is not saved or sent to the public site. Reloading requires reconnecting. Do not use your Instagram password.
